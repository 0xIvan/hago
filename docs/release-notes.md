- Hago has a new look built around your day as a ring, like the app icon. Overview shows today from 4am to 4am at a glance: work and personal blocks around the ring, what's still ahead as dots, and your total in the middle. Hover a block to see what it was and how long it lasted.
- Overview now leads with what you want to know: what you're on right now, how today splits between work and personal, where the time went (with app and site icons), and a bar for each day of this week.
- The sidebar always shows today's ring and total, with a one-click pause.
- The menu bar shows today's ring, your work and personal time, and what you're on now.
- Refreshed colours for Work, Personal and Not filed yet, used the same way across Overview, Activity, Review and Reports.

Download the universal DMG, open it and drag Hago to Applications. The app and DMG are Developer ID signed and notarized. Requires macOS 14 or newer; supports Apple Silicon and Intel.

Existing data stays local in the Hago application-support folder. If Accessibility stays unavailable after upgrading from an older locally signed build, remove Hago from the Accessibility / Device Control and Data Access list in System Settings, add `/Applications/Hago.app` again, and enable its switch.
