Hago tracks computer time, including reading and watching without input, and helps categorize it with starter work profiles, remembered corrections and optional AI suggestions.

- Durable recording checkpoints, pause/resume and editable activity time.
- Unknown apps go to Needs review; existing history and personal rules are preserved.
- Resumable setup, work profiles, correction previews and persistent undo.
- Optional direct AI connections alongside existing CLI integrations.
- Open at Login, manual Check for Updates, and corrected report periods/comparisons.
- Restored the compact `2:46 | 11:50` menu bar format.

Download the universal DMG, open it and drag Hago to Applications. The app and DMG are Developer ID signed and notarized. Requires macOS 14 or newer; supports Apple Silicon and Intel.

Existing data stays local in the Hago application-support folder. The bundle identity is now `com.ivan.hago`, so macOS may require fresh Accessibility and Chrome Automation grants. Sleep, lock and pause stop tracking; unattended time on an awake, unlocked Mac remains editable in Activity.
