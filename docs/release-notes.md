- Review is built around AI doing the work. The header says in one line how much is left and where AI stands: checking now, not looked at yet (with the next automatic check), or done and waiting for you.
- Items are sorted into "AI has a pick", "AI left these to you" and "Not checked by AI yet", so it's always clear whether AI has seen something.
- When AI has a pick, its choice is the highlighted button on the row. Clicking it, or pressing A, accepts the pick. "Accept N AI Picks" accepts every Work and Personal pick at once.
- One button at most: Check with AI, Accept AI Picks, or Ask Again. The sort options and the separate AI banner are gone, and the biggest items come first.
- Your last choice (with Undo) and anything AI filed automatically in the past day share one quiet line under the header.
- Rule suggestions from your own choices now sit at the bottom under "Make it automatic".

Download the universal DMG, open it and drag Hago to Applications. The app and DMG are Developer ID signed and notarized. Requires macOS 14 or newer; supports Apple Silicon and Intel.

Existing data stays local in the Hago application-support folder. If Accessibility stays unavailable after upgrading from an older locally signed build, remove Hago from the Accessibility / Device Control and Data Access list in System Settings, add `/Applications/Hago.app` again, and enable its switch.
