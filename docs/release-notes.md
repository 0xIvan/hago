## Codex recovery

- Reconnect Codex verifies sign-in and shows the next step in AI suggestions.
- Reconnecting keeps your existing sharing permission. First-time setup shows what Hago shares before asking for permission.
- Check Review shows progress, offers a retry if it fails, and opens Review when the check is complete.

Download the universal DMG, open it, and drag Hago to Applications. Hago requires macOS 14 or later and supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
